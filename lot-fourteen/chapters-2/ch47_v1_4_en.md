# Chapter 47: Clause eleven
*Lot Fourteen* · Version 1.4 · EN

## Day Three Hundred and Seven · Thursday 6 August

He decided on the Wednesday night that he was not going to tell her first, and he wrote the reason down before he did it rather than afterward, because a reason written afterward is not a reason.

---

*Not because she would stop me. Because the moment I put it in front of her it becomes hers.*

*What I am about to build is the first thing this year that will exist on paper with her name nowhere on it.*

*I will tell her when it is done and before it is sent. That is the arrangement I am making with myself at half past eleven on a Wednesday, alone, and I am writing it here so that it is a promise and not an intention.*

---

The house on the river has been winding up since April.

---

There are no settlements. There is no desk and no glass panel and no chair on the visitor's side, and there is a man on the third floor with two crates and a schedule of things to be closed.

---

And it still keeps a permanent series.

---

That is the whole of what Georgij worked out on the Thursday morning, and it took him eleven weeks to arrive at, and it had been sitting in front of him the entire time.

---

A house that is winding up is still a house. Its terms of business bind it until somebody signs the series over to an archive or a successor, and until that morning it has to answer for what it holds. Nobody has told Georgij when that morning is.

---

Nobody has told Hwang either, because a fund decides that and a fund is in no hurry. It is the only reason this is possible in August, and there is no version in which it is still possible in the spring.

---

Mrs Sunwoo told him about the series in October, in a ballroom, without any idea what she was handing over. The new man tidied everything. One series, the whole thirty years in one sequence. Before him you could not find a lot from four years ago without knowing which of four systems it had been entered under, and now a girl of nineteen can find anything in the place in about a minute.

---

Georgij has thought of that as an obstacle for ten months. It is the reason the fourth line is a strike-through in a book that will outlive everybody, and a man who cannot be bribed cannot be gone round.

---

He sat at the desk in the small room on Thursday morning and turned it over for the first time.

---

A man who has built a system that cannot be gone round has built a system that cannot decline to answer.

---

The terms of business are eleven pages and Annie has a copy, because everybody who buys at that house signs a copy, and she has kept every piece of paper she has ever signed in a drawer that locks.

---

He asked Mrs Seo for the key at ten past nine and she gave it to him without asking why, which is a thing he thought about for the rest of the day.

---

It took him under a minute to find the file and he stopped with his hand on it.

---

October. Seven pages of conditions and a schedule at the back, and on the schedule, in a column, four figures and a lot number.

---

He has never read it.

---

He has known it existed since about the fourth day. He has walked past that drawer perhaps four hundred times. There has never been an hour in ten months in which he could not have opened it. He has told himself, when he thought about it at all, that there was nothing in it he did not already know.

---

He read it at the desk with the door open, because closing it would have made it a different act.

---

Most of it is a contract for the sale of a thing, and he had expected that, and it was still not the same as expecting it.

---

Clause four is about the condition of the item on delivery.

Clause six is about what the house will and will not warrant.

Clause nine is the one about the register, and the strike-through, and the fee, and it is three lines long and perfectly clear and it is why Byun had a price.

---

Clause eleven is about provenance.

---

*Where a bona fide interest is asserted in respect of any lot, the House shall upon written request supply a statement of record. The statement shall be prepared from the permanent series and shall be entered in the permanent series under the reference of the lot to which it relates. No statement shall be withheld on the ground that the record is nil.*

---

Georgij read that four times.

---

Then he sat back in a chair in a small room on the river and did the thing he does about once a year. He laughed at something that is not funny to anybody else and would not survive being explained.

---

The last sentence is the whole of it and it was written by a man who was proud of himself.

---

He put it into his own words on the page, because a sentence you have rewritten is a sentence you have understood.

---

*If somebody with a proper interest asks the house what it holds on a lot, the house has to answer in writing. The answer goes into the permanent series, under that lot, for ever. And the house is not allowed to stay silent just because the answer is nothing.*

---

*He wrote that clause so that nobody could ever again say the house had hidden a record. It also means the house cannot hide the absence of one.*

---

*A strike-through is a mark on a page and anybody can argue about what it means. A statement of record, prepared under clause eleven and entered in the permanent series, saying that as at a date in August the House holds no entry in respect of that lot, is a document. It is the house saying it, in its own series and its own hand, at a date after the sale. It cannot be taken out again, because the man who built the series built it so that nothing can.*

---

*That is not evidence of what happened to her. It is evidence that she was there and is not, and that is the thing nobody has been able to write down since the spring four years ago.*

---

The rest of Thursday was the hard part, and it was hard in the way that only writing is.

---

The request has to be from somebody with a bona fide interest, and it has to be about the right lot, and it has to be impossible to answer any way except the one way.

---

He drafted it eleven times.

---

The first four were too long, in the manner of a man who wants to be understood. The fifth asked two questions and would have got one answer. The eighth mentioned a date that gave away that the writer already knew something, and he burned that one in the grate in the small room and did not keep the ash.

---

The eleventh is four sentences.

---

It does not say who is interested or why. Clause eleven does not require it and a request that explains itself invites a reply that argues with the explanation.

It does not ask what happened to the lot. It asks the house to supply a statement of record.

It names the lot by its reference in the permanent series, which he has, because Byun sold four of them and Hwang found the four fees. A house that renumbered thirty years into one sequence gave every lot in its history a permanent number, including the ones it had already been paid to forget.

And it gives an address for the reply that is not this house and never has been.

---

He read the four sentences back at about seven in the evening and then sat with the page for a while, because something in it was not right and it was not the drafting.

---

*Her terms of business. Her copy. Her drawer, opened with a key her housekeeper handed me without asking why. Her lot number.*

*I wrote at half past eleven last night that this is the first thing this year with her name nowhere on it. There is not one part of it that is not hers. The only thing I have added is the four sentences and the address for the reply.*

---

He did not cross the line out, because a reason written on Wednesday is a record of what a man thought on Wednesday.

---

He wrote the correction underneath it instead.

---

*Nothing here was built without her. It is the first thing this year that will not have her name on the paper, and I have got that by taking every other part of it out of her house. I would like that noticed by somebody, and the only person who could notice it is the one I am not going to tell until Friday.*

* * *

The address is the part that cost him something.

---

A request under clause eleven has to come from a person, and Georgij is not one in any sense that a letterhead recognises. He owns nothing and he is on nothing. The only paper in this country with his name on it exists because a compliance officer needed it in the second week of October.

---

He telephoned Mr Yeom at four in the afternoon and did not go, and did not eat anything, and it took nine minutes.

"I am going to read you four sentences," said Georgij. "You are going to say yes or no, and if it is no it is no, and I am not going to improve it and ring back."

"Read them."

---

He read them.

---

There was a pause on the line.

"Change *supply* to *furnish*," said Mr Yeom.

"Why."

"Because *supply* is what a buyer says and *furnish* is what the clause says, and the man who wrote that clause reads his own words back to himself when they arrive on somebody else's paper." Something moved on the other end. "He will answer a letter that sounds like his own drafting inside three days. He will hand a letter that sounds like a customer to somebody junior, and it will sit for a fortnight."

"Then it is furnish."

"It is furnish, and it goes on my letterhead, and I will sign it tonight and post it myself in the morning from a box that is not near my house." He said it flatly. "And I am going to write nothing on it that you have not read to me. If he telephones me I will say that I am a director of the fund that owns the house and that I am entitled to ask, which is true, and then I will stop talking."

"That is the correct amount to say."

"I know it is. I have been in this trade my whole life and I have only ever been bad at the beginning of things." He paused. "How long have you been sitting with those four sentences."

"Since about eleven o'clock."

"Then go and eat something and let a sixty-three-year-old man do the posting."

* * *

He told her at seven, in the small room, standing, with the eleven pages on the desk beside two dozen white stems in two vases.

"I have done a thing and not told you first," said Georgij.

---

Annie looked at the papers before she looked at him, which told him she had recognised them from the door.

"That is my October file."

"It is."

"You have never opened that drawer."

"I have never opened that drawer, and I opened it at ten past nine this morning. Mrs Seo gave me the key without asking me anything, and I have been thinking about that all day and I still do not know what to do with it."

---

Annie came and stood at the corner of the desk.

"Say what you found."

---

He told her, in order, and it took under four minutes, and he did not leave out the eleven drafts or the one he had burned.

---

She read clause eleven herself, twice, with one finger under the last sentence.

"He wrote that so that nobody could say the house had hidden anything," she said.

"Yes."

"And it is going to be the thing that ends the house."

"I think so."

---

Annie put the page down and did not say anything for a while.

"Now say the other part," she said. "You did not come up here at seven to tell me about a clause."

"No." He kept his hands at his sides. "I decided last night that I was not going to bring it to you first, and I wrote down why before I did it rather than afterward. I am going to say the reason out loud even though it will sound better than it was."

"Say it anyway."

"Because the moment it is in this room it is yours. This is the first thing I have built this year with your name nowhere on it, and it cannot be that and also have been discussed in your library." He did not move. "And the second half is that I wanted to do one entirely by myself, and I am not sure which of the two came first, and I have written both down in that order."

---

Annie looked at him for what was probably four seconds.

"Good," she said.

---

He had not expected that and it took him a moment.

"What you mean by good."

"I mean that I have been waiting since March for you to do something without bringing it to me first. I noticed that you did not bring it, and I noticed that you came up here at seven and told me before it went in a post box." She put one hand flat on the eleven pages. "You did not ask me and you did not hide it. Those are not the same thing and everybody in this business gets them confused."

"Then you are not going to tell me not to send it."

"I am going to tell you one thing about it and it is not about whether." Annie turned the file round on the desk so that the schedule at the back was facing him. "You read this today."

"I read it today."

"All of it."

"All of it," said Georgij. "Clause four is about the condition of the item on delivery."

---

Neither of them said anything.

"Sit down," said Annie.

"I would rather not, and I am going to say why, because it is the only thing I have got left today that is true and not tidy." He stayed exactly where he was. "If I sit down I am going to want to talk about clause four, and I am not able to do that tonight, and I would like to still be able to do it in September."

---

Annie took her hand off the papers.

"Then it keeps until September," she said, "and I will still be here, and I am saying that as a fact and not as a kindness."

* * *

He wrote it up at half past nine, on the first page, and it is four lines long.

---

*Clause eleven. The house has to furnish a statement of record on request and the statement goes into the permanent series and cannot be withheld because the record is nil.*

*Requested today. Yeom posts it in the morning from a box away from his house. He changed one word and the word was right.*

*Nobody gave me this. I went and got it out of a drawer in this house that I have walked past four hundred times.*

---

And then, on the second page, because the second page gets everything:

---

*Mrs Seo, since ten past nine this morning, and she is not on this page because she found something out. She is on it because she handed a man a key without asking him a single question, and I have not decided whether that is trust or nine years of a household learning not to.*
