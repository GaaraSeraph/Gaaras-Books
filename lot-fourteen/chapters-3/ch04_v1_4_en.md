# Chapter 4: Sixteen
*Lot Fourteen* · Version 1.4 · EN

## Day Six Hundred and Forty-Five · Saturday 10 July

There was a car on the drive at twenty past ten that had not been booked through the house.

---

Georgij noticed it from the landing window, and what he noticed was not the car.

It was that Mrs Seo was already at the front door before it stopped, which she does not do, because the door gets answered when it is knocked on.

* * *

He went down.

---

There was a boy in the hall.

* * *

He is about one metre seventy-two and he has grown into most of it. He is wearing a suit that fits, which means somebody paid a tailor and not a shop, and shoes that have been worn in on purpose.

---

He was standing in the middle of the hall with his hands at his sides.

He had been in the house for perhaps forty seconds.

---

In that time he had looked at two doors off the hall, at the staircase, at the glass wall at the end and the hill behind it, and at the two people in front of him.

He had done all of it without moving his head more than he had to.

Then he stopped and stood still, because he had finished.

* * *

It took him about nine seconds.

---

Georgij stood on the bottom step and did not say anything for longer than he meant to.

* * *

"This is Lee Jun," said Mrs Seo.

---

She said it the way she says the second sitting is at seven.

"He is sixteen. He came from a house in Yangju and he has an account of himself in a folder, and I have read it, and it is in order."

"Thank you."

"The room at the top of the back stairs is made up and it has been since Thursday." She had her hands folded in front of her. "I was told on Thursday."

---

Georgij heard the whole of that sentence.

Mrs Seo has never once told him a thing she was not told to tell him, and she has never once left one of them out either.

---

She went back down the passage to whatever she had been doing before the car turned in.

* * *

"Good morning," said Georgij.

---

"Good morning, sir."

The boy said it in the register a boy of sixteen uses on a man he has been told about and has not met.

It was correct, and it was the third thing he had got right in a minute and a half.

---

"You counted the doors."

"Yes, sir."

"So did I. I did it standing where you are standing, and it took me the same time." Georgij did not soften any of it. "You may stop doing it in this house. It will take you about four months to stop, and nobody is going to mind while you are still doing it."

---

Lee Jun did not say anything to that.

He had been given a piece of information he had no use for, and he could not tell whether it was a test, and he did what he has learned to do, which is nothing.

* * *

There was one bag on the step behind him and the driver had already gone.

---

It is a good bag and it is not full. There is nothing in this house that came out of it and there is not going to be.

A boy of sixteen who has lived somewhere for two years has photographs, or a ticket stub, or something a person gave him.

There is none of that in it, and that is a decision, and somebody made it before the car turned in.

* * *

The folder was on the table in the hall where a folder goes.

---

Georgij read it standing up, and it took eleven minutes, and neither of them sat down.

It is a name, a registration, and a guardianship order made two years ago and renewed once.

There is a training contract with a firm in Yangju that trains house staff for people who can afford to have staff trained, and a certificate for the year he finished, seventeen months late and top of nine.

There is a health record with four entries in it and a dentist twice a year.

---

There is a line in the guardianship order for the person who applied, and it is a solicitor in Jung-gu acting on instruction, and there is no instructing party named anywhere in the folder.

Georgij read that page twice.

---

Everything in it is correct.

Somebody has spent two years making a person legal, one document at a time, and has done it properly and has not put a name on any of it.

* * *

"Do you know who paid for the training."

"No, sir."

---

"Do you want to."

Lee Jun took slightly longer than he had taken over anything else.

"No, sir."

---

"That is the first thing you have said this morning that you decided yourself."

* * *

The boy looked at him then, properly, for about a second, and what went across his face was not gratitude.

---

It was that he had been caught, and that he had not expected to be caught in this house, and that he was now going to have to work out what kind of house it is.

* * *

Georgij took him up to the room at the top of the back stairs and did not carry anything for him.

---

He showed him the bathroom, the back door, the kitchen, and the two places in the garden where a person can stand and not be seen from any window.

He did not explain the last one and the boy did not ask.

---

Halfway along the upstairs passage Georgij understood that he was not showing him anything.

Lee Jun was checking it against something he already had.

---

Somebody gave a training firm in Yangju the arrangement of this house, and they did it long enough ago for a boy to have learned it.

---

At the door of the room Lee Jun stopped and looked at the collar.

He had been not looking at it for eleven minutes and he had done it well.

"You may look at it."

---

"Yes, sir."

He looked at it for about two seconds, which was as long as he needed, and then he looked at the floor.

---

Two years ago that boy had a line in a catalogue and a number over it. He has none now.

The man showing him the room has neither, and has the collar.

"It does not come off," said Georgij. "Nobody in this house will ask you to wear anything and nobody will explain that one to you. If you want to know what it is for, ask me in a year and I will tell you then."

"Yes, sir."

* * *

The boy had his hand on the door and did not open it.

---

"May I ask one thing, sir?"

"Ask."

"What am I paid?"

---

He asked it flatly and without any apology in it, and it is the only question he asked all day, and it is the correct one.

A boy who has been sold twice does not need to be told what he is worth. He needs to be told what he is owed, and they are not the same number, and he has worked out that they are not.

---

Georgij did not have the answer.

He has never been paid anything and he has never once been asked, and it took him about two seconds to find the door out of that.

"Mrs Seo pays the household and she pays it on the last working day of the month." He said it in her voice and not in his. "Ask her on Monday. She will have expected it and she will not think less of you for it."

"Yes, sir."

* * *

Georgij went down the back stairs and stood in the passage for a moment.

---

He is twenty-seven.

At sixteen he was in the sixth house, or the seventh, and he has never had to work out which because there has never been a reason to.

He worked it out standing in that passage. It was the seventh.

He did not do anything with it.

* * *

Annie was at the desk at half past two and had been since eleven.

---

"There is a boy in the room at the top of the back stairs," said Georgij.

"Yes."

"His folder is in order. All of it. Two years of it."

"It is."

---

Georgij stood where he stands.

"On the fourth of October I asked you what had happened to lot eleven. I asked again on the seventh." He said it without any weight on it at all. "You did not answer either time, and I stopped."

"You did."

* * *

Annie put the pen down.

---

"Ask it now."

"I am not going to ask it now. It is answered." Georgij had his hands behind his back. "I would like to say what I have got instead, and then I am going to go and see about the second sitting, because it is Saturday and Mrs Seo is short."

"Say it."

---

"You bought a person in November of the first year to do me a favour, and you did it before I had said three hundred words to you." He did not look away. "And you did it properly, and you have gone on doing it properly for two years, and you did not tell me, and I do not know which of those four things I am supposed to do something with."

* * *

Annie did not answer for a while.

---

"The first three are mine," she said. "The fourth one is the only one you are entitled to and you have just spent it."

"Yes, Mistress."

"That is not agreement. Say whether it is."

---

Georgij thought about that for about four seconds, which is a long time for him.

"No," he said.

* * *

Annie looked at him.

---

"Good," she said. "Then we are going to leave it where it is, and it is going to be there in ten years, and neither of us is going to be surprised when it comes up."

"Yes."

"He is not a present and he is not a debt. He was fourteen and he was for sale twice." She picked the pen back up. "What you do with him from here is yours, and I am not going to have an opinion about it, and you may take that as the whole of what I owe you on this."

---

"I do not know what I am going to do with him."

"No."

"I know two things. One of them is this house and the other one is the trade." Georgij did not move. "There is not a third."

Annie did not offer him one.

* * *

Georgij went and saw about the second sitting.

---

At seven the tray went up the way it has gone up at seven for ten years, and there was an extra place laid at the kitchen table, and nobody had asked Mrs Seo to lay it.

---

Lee Jun sat where he was put and ate what he was given and said four words in an hour, and all four of them were to Mrs Seo.

He has been in the house for nine hours.

He has not once been in a room without knowing where the door is.
