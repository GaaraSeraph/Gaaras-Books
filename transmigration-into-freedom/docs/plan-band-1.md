=== 1. WHAT BAND 1 IS ===

Thirty-five chapters, from the ash to the first quarter. The spine in one
sentence: a man who believed control can only be held alone builds, in about
six weeks, a thing that holds without him - and the volume ends the first
time he can fall over and nothing falls with him.

That is Stage 6 of character-arc.md exactly ("not 'nobody can take it from
me' but 'it does not collapse if I fall over'"), and it is four chapters,
not a mood. Every one of them carries its own irreversible event
(CLAUDE.md, no preparation chapters; the test is run in section 6).

Where the volume ends: the evening the first quarter is paid and receipted,
the board on the forge wall carries its first written number, and the system
finally files him.

Stages audit against character-arc.md section 2:
- Stages 1 to 4: delivered and logged (milestones in the tracker; Stage 4's
  "works in his absence" paid in ch25 by Marit).
- Stage 5 ("he wins something through the politics he despises, and has to
  decide who he becomes if he is good at it"): the winning is paid in ch26
  (write us down) and ch31 (the rent instead of the account). The "who he
  becomes" half is exactly what 32 to 34 make him look at, so it closes
  inside the finale rather than before it.
- Stage 4's counter-pressure ("someone fails him, badly, and the old rule
  gets its best evidence in years") was never paid. It is NOT paid in Band 1
  either - see section 5, fork 5.


=== 2. WHERE CH31 LEAVES THE BOARD ===

The obligations the last four chapters inherit. Each with its source, so
nothing gets invented and nothing gets dropped.

1. THE RENT LETTER IS EAST (ch31). Six lines, agrees the ruin is held of
   the lord, proposes a fixed annual rent in place of an account, payable
   in advance, the first ready at the quarter. Unanswered.
2. THE LEVY COUNTER IS EAST SINCE ch26, on Gunnar's tablet: not six names
   off a roll, a company engaged for a season, for a stated thing, at a
   stated price. Gunnar could not engage anything and said so. The company
   he wrote down is HADES - a Staple trading house of one man. The crew's
   fighting company exists on no Roll anywhere (bible 6c), and the bible
   promised that a man in two registers gets remarked on "long before Gaara
   wants it remarked on". That bill arrives in ch32.
3. THE SHED MUST OPEN to raise the rent in advance, which breaks the price
   Runa built and he paid for three times (ch30, ch31). Chosen out loud,
   not yet done.
4. YLVA'S TERM (ch31): the number written every quarter, on a board, on
   the wall, where Bern can see it. The board does not exist yet.
5. THE QUARTER ITSELF: "the bearer of the roll will attend" (ch31). Who
   bears the roll is the standing question - bible section 0 already holds
   the ch32 working title.
6. RUNNING: Ketil's year (ch30). Marit holds the valley (ch30). Hades
   stands on a lease it can lose (ch31). Kills 16, Level 7, NOTHING
   banked since the ch28 spend. MP untouched and still not his to use.
7. RESERVED AND ARMED: the first soldier read. The level scale (bible
   section 2) was built low on purpose so that "the first time he reads a
   serjeant he gets a number beside which Ylva is a child", and it said do
   not spend it early. Thirty-one chapters of a world that stops at 16
   have paid for it. It is no longer early. It lands in ch32.
8. LOADED TWICE AND PROMISED: Class None (ch28 makes it the title and the
   fear, ch29 puts it next to Bearer of record: none, ch30 ends on "one of
   these days"). A promise this size belongs to a volume end. It pays in
   ch35 - section 5, fork 3.


=== 3. THE FOUR CHAPTERS ===

Per chapter: the event, what lands, what it must not do, mechanics, title
proposal. Titles from ch5 on promise rather than describe (bible section 0);
all four titles here are proposals, the author names the chapters.

--- CH32 - THE QUARTER ---
Working note in bible section 0: "the quarter, and who attends with the
roll". Title proposal: PAID IN ADVANCE.

THE EVENT: the lord takes the rent. Money crosses a table and is entered
in the lord's book, and for the first time in eight hundred years the
valley holds a receipt from power instead of an absence.

THE RUN-UP IS A PAGE, NOT A CHAPTER. The shed opened days ago, the price
broke exactly as Runa said it would, the sum got raised at broken prices
and every coin of the damage is self-inflicted and was chosen in ch31.
Compress it and set it against the quarter itself. Ylva is in the Wold as
half-owner - the first number for her board is counted out in front of
her before it is ever written on a wall.

WHO ATTENDS WITH THE ROLL: not Gunnar. The lord's steward - the man who
HAS what Gunnar said he did not have ("I cannot engage anything. I do not
have that."). Gunnar is present and smaller than his own tablet, and he is
not humiliated for it: his report held, which is why the steward is here
at all. With the steward, an escort.

THE RESERVED BEAT LANDS HERE, and it costs no setup, exactly as designed:
Gaara reads his first professional soldiers. Proposal: the two escort
serjeants at 14 and 15 - bored men at a door, and either of them would go
through the entire crew that took three trips into a dead city. Ylva, the
highest number forty houses ever produced, would be the third most
dangerous person at that door. And the steward himself reads as a SIX,
which is the better half of the beat: the most powerful man in the room is
an ordinary body with a book, because administration, not muscle, is what
runs a world - the book's thesis delivered as a reading, not a speech.

WHY THE RENT IS TAKEN: the ledger strategy (bible 6b, option 1) pays off
as designed. The true version has been in the record for weeks - found
open, shut by locals at their own cost, contained, gives nothing back -
plus a written rent offer that argues with nothing. Taking the rent is
the path of least paperwork; refusing it means reversing a record two of
his own men signed, and then owning whatever comes out of that gate.
Gaara watches a man read his file and agree with it, and understands that
he has just been governed well, and hates how much he admires it.

THE STING, and it forces ch33: the steward reads the roll aloud before he
closes it. Written against the hole stands a company - and the company on
the tablet is Hades, a merchants' house of one man in the Staple. A lord
does not engage a trading house to stand in front of a hole in a hill;
fighting engagements run on the Roll, warranted, with sureties. So the
choice comes back with a clock on it: by the spring levy there is a
warranted company standing where the tablet says one stands, or there are
six names. He does not threaten. He corrects the filing.

MUST NOT: no villain. The steward is Gunnar's read scaled up - a ledger,
a superior, an appetite for not being blamed - and he is COMPETENT, which
is scarier and rarer. No fight with lord's men, not in this book. Runa is
not spent here: if she appears, it is one exchange about the broken price
she predicted, and it stays dry.

MECHANICS: no level, nothing spent, nothing banked to spend. The system
content of the chapter is the soldier read - a revelation, not a gain,
same class as ch21's scale detonation. Kills stay 16.

--- CH33 - THE CHARTER ---
Title proposal: A NAME HE DID NOT CHOOSE.

THE EVENT: the crew becomes a warranted company. Names into the Roll's
book, a goat paid, two sureties liable, and none of it can be unwritten.

THE FOUR SENTENCES DOWN A ROAD get spent here (bible 6b flagged the beat
and said do not waste it; the display never needed it, this does). Gaara
sends the QUESTION to the valley, not the instruction - a charter binds
the people who sign it, so it is theirs to refuse - written, because two
people in nine can read and one of them is holding the valley. What comes
back is Marit and four of the crew on the road to the Wold, carrying the
answer in her hand: yes, and the name they chose without him.

HE LEARNS THE NAME STANDING IN THE ROLL HOUSE, as news, in front of
strangers, which is exactly how the bible said it must arrive (6c: Marit
or Bern names it, never him; it is theirs, they earned it in a dead
street in ch17). Name: fork 2, section 5.

MARIT IS CAPTAIN. Not his gift and not a promotion scene - the charter
writes down what has been true since ch25, when she ran the city twice
without him and improved the doctrine doing it. She is in the valley; he
is a man who has to be in a town. The Roll requires a captain, and there
is exactly one honest answer.

AND HIS OWN NAME GOES DOWN AS SURETY. A voucher here is not a reference,
it is liability (6c): if the company defaults, the sureties pay. So the
man whose rule was that other people's failures become your failures
signs himself liable, in a book, for a company he does not command - the
old rule inverted in one line of ink, and he does not explain himself,
because he does not explain himself. Second surety: fork 4.

ALSO IN THE ROOM: Signe writes five names and asks how each is spelled,
and enjoys none of it out loud. Sivert's two coins go into the roll at
member rate. The clerk-outranks-the-captain reading from ch21 is now
something Marit gets to discover for herself, and she and Signe
understand each other inside a minute, which unsettles him more than the
serjeants did.

MUST NOT: no ceremony. The Roll is a counter and a book, and the book
already taught us its whole poetry in ch21 (two lines, one of them a
date). Marit does not defer to him in that room and nobody remarks on it.
No romance beat between the institutions - the hall is not the place.

MECHANICS: none. The goat and the two coins move the purse, not the
sheet. Kills 16.

--- CH34 - THE FIRST JOB ---
Title proposal: TAKE IT AT A WALL.

THE EVENT: the company kills under seal for the first time - paid
violence, witnessed for the lord - and the doctrine leaves the valley in
a stranger's copy.

THE SHAPE: the steward does not buy sight unseen. Before the engagement
is sealed he sends what he trusts - one serjeant - to see what his lord
would be paying for. A hunt in the city, under observation: the stated
thing from the ch26 pitch (hold the valley, keep the gate shut, the count
delivered at each quarter) done in front of the man whose trade this is.

MARIT COMMANDS IT. Gaara stands IN the line, takes an order in front of a
professional soldier, and does what it says - talking the entire time,
because obedience has never once made him quiet. That is the arc's final
statement made as behaviour: he built the structure, installed the
captain, and now stands inside it as a part that can be replaced. The
serjeant watches a 15 would-not-believe-it: farmers between 4 and 8
taking a thing above their weight with plumbing, rules, and a wall, and
nobody dying. Bern counts them up and counts them down.

AND THE DOCTRINE TRAVELS. The serjeant reads the byre wall - or has the
six lines read to him - and copies them onto wax to take east, because a
man who trains men recognises doctrine when he finally meets some. Bern's
name goes east at the top of it, first name on the first written doctrine
this world has produced. Nobody is paid and nobody is asked: Gaara
watches his first book get copied by exactly the customer section 7
names, before he owns a press, and files the lesson instead of the
grievance. NOBODY SAYS THE WORD PUBLISHING, and nobody works out the Roll
runs on copies - that stays unsaid, per 6c. This plants Band 2 and closes
nothing.

THE SEAL: on the serjeant's word the engagement concludes - the company,
for the season, for the stated thing, at a stated price. The report
writes itself in one line, which is what Gaara promised Gunnar in ch26,
and now it has been true twice and a lord's man said it first.

MUST NOT: Gaara takes NO kill in this chapter, and it is load-bearing
twice over. Once as the bookend - ch17 stood him still through eleven
minutes watching his counter refuse to move, and the volume closes the
frame with him standing still on purpose, at peace with the arithmetic
that used to make him complain. And once mechanically: the ch28
Roofwalker already put ONE kill with a blade over forty centimetres,
above his level, on the books. A second fires the fixed two-kill rule
and issues a third weapon skill, and the bible says no third category by
accident. If a blade-category skill is ever to fire, it fires on purpose
in Band 2, not as a side effect of the finale.

MECHANICS: crew kills move, his stay 16. No level, nothing spent. MP is
not used and not drawn - the drum stays home.

--- CH35 - THE FILING ---
Title proposal: WHAT HE ACTUALLY IS.

THE EVENT: the system files him. The class arrives.

THE SETTING: Oldstep, the evening the first quarter-number goes onto the
board. One pass through the running machine, none of it needing him: Ylva
writes the number on the wall where Bern can see it, exactly as she
demanded it; the doctrine hangs on the byre with a hole where the
serjeant's copy pressed through; the drum turns on his purse; Ketil is a
year less three weeks from being seen; the company has a name he did not
choose and a captain who is not him. The village told him the news of the
week in the road before he had his pack off, because it is their valley
and he only lives there.

AND THEN THE PANEL OPENS, unasked, in the voice it uses for livestock, at
the least ceremonial moment available - proposal: while he is doing
something entirely ordinary, alone for the first time in four chapters.
Seven levels it has scored his hands and never once his thinking (ch28),
and now it has watched him register, charter, arbitrate, price, teach,
and put a valley on a roll, and the condition is met: the choice is
obvious and narrow, and he dislikes the options (thread 5, exactly as the
tracker demanded).

[ CLASS AVAILABLE ] - and every option is a kind of clerk. Proposal:
STEWARD, FACTOR, CLERK. Sixteen kills, a blade named Invoice on his hip,
and the machine offers him three flavours of paperwork. He takes STEWARD,
cold, in one move, with the reason stated - the ch12, ch24, ch28 spend
pattern, one last time. Because it is true: everything he has, he holds
of somebody else. The ruin of the lord. The forge, half of Ylva. The
company, of Marit. The valley was never his at all. A steward is a man
who runs what he does not own, it is the job title of the class of man he
fenced with all volume, and the panel has filed him correctly and
unkindly, which is the only way it has ever filed anything.
Fork 3 for the alternatives, including not paying this in Band 1 at all.

WHAT A CLASS DOES: NOT REVEALED. One line of system output at most, or
nothing - Band 2 opens on it. The volume pays the promise of WHAT he is,
not the mechanics of it.

THE LAST PAGE: control, redefined, said as behaviour. He lies down to
sleep with nothing in his hands - no watch to keep, no count to run,
other people holding every load he used to sleep on top of - and it is
the mirror of the mat behind the desk in ch1, and NOTHING POINTS AT IT.
He went to sleep once and woke up owning nothing in a world that owed him
nothing. He goes to sleep now owning nothing in a valley that would
notice by morning if he did not wake, and that is the whole difference,
and the book does not say so out loud. Morning comes. Band 1 ends.

MUST NOT: no speech about growth, his or anybody's. The class beat does
not land during or next to a sex scene (the panel stays out of beds,
ch13). The origin of his own name does NOT land here - see section 4. No
DiGiorno shape anywhere near the class reveal, it is the exact trap.

MECHANICS: the class is the volume's mechanical climax and its only
change. Level 7, Kills 16, MP 60 untouched by him, Dagger Mastery D,
Improvised Weapon Handling E, and a class where None stood for
thirty-five chapters.

=== 5. FORKS - THE AUTHOR DECIDES ===

Numbered so an answer can be one line. The plan stands on the
recommendation until he says otherwise.

1. DOES THE LORD TAKE THE RENT AT THE QUARTER? Plan says YES - the ledger
   strategy paying off is the 6b design working, and the cost was already
   paid in ch31 with the shed. The alternative (the account is forced,
   open conflict with the lord) breaks the volume shape and moves Band 1's
   ending into Band 2's war. Not recommended.
2. DECIDED BY THE AUTHOR: the fighting company is SERAPH. From Gaara,
   like Hades - the pair is the design (bible 6c: the one below who
   keeps, the burning one who stands in front, and the flame mark loop).
   The earlier candidates (Nine Came Down, The Wall, Oldstep) are
   discarded; the crew-names-it rule is superseded. Open at writing
   time: only the staging - when and to whom he says the name first.
3. THE CLASS. (a) It pays in ch35: recommended, a volume should close a
   promise this loud, and Band 2 opens on what it does. Name STEWARD
   recommended; FACTOR and CLERK as the disliked alternatives on the
   panel. (b) It stays None past Band 1 and ch35 closes on the empty box
   again: legitimate, crueller, and it wastes the best chapter-title of
   the four. If (b), ch35 needs a new event and this plan reopens.
4. THE SECOND SURETY next to Gaara. Recommended ORDULF - the metal buys
   the vouching and the vouching legitimises the metal, exactly the 6c
   trade, and a man who walked two days to not-look-at-Bern will sign for
   Bern's company and deny that is why. Alternative EGIL, who has vouched
   once already and took the test instead of the assurance.
5. THE UNPAID COUNTER-PRESSURE ("someone fails him, badly"). Recommended:
   carry it to Band 2 whole. Band 1's ledger is full, and a betrayal
   forced into the finale would be arithmetic, not story. If the author
   wants it in Band 1 after all, the only honest slot is ch34 under the
   serjeant's eyes - somebody breaks Bern's rule under observation - and
   it costs the engagement its cleanness, which is a real price and a
   real option.
6. THE RENT FIGURE. Set at writing time, in goats, not invented here.
   Constraints it must satisfy: it consumes roughly the shed at broken
   prices plus the quarter's earnings (so the year starts near zero); it
   is "considerably more than a share is worth" (ch31 says so); and
   paying it AGAIN next year is only possible if the company's season
   pays - which is Band 2's opening tension and must not be solved in
   advance.
7. THE STEWARD'S NAME AND THE SERJEANTS' NUMBERS. Names at writing time
   (proposal for the steward: Audun). The numbers 14, 15 and the
   steward's 6 are proposals inside the fixed scale (soldiers 12 to 18);
   whatever is written becomes canon and goes into the bible with its
   chapter tag.
